- Two labs per week, on Wednesdays and Thursdays, 1.5 hours each.
  - Parallel to the theoretical classes

## Evaluation

- A project contributing 50% of the final grade is due in December (exact date and guidelines to be announced).
- Group size is strictly limited to 4-6 members (penalties apply for non-compliance).
- The project involves selecting and analyzing a 'big dataset' of your choice, with some flexibility in the scope and approach. This will be explained in more detail in the following weeks.
